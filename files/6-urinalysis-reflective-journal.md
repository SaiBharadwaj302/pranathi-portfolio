# Urinalysis Rotation: Reflective Journal

**Cambridge Health Alliance (CHA), a Harvard Medical School affiliate, Cambridge, MA**

**Instruments:** Sysmex UD, Clinitek Novus, manual microscopy

---

## Overview

My urinalysis experience at CHA was overall really good and helpful. I felt like the theory I had already learned made a big difference when I actually started working with the samples.

One thing I found really interesting was learning about the demographics of samples and how common or uncommon certain findings are. It made me realize that not everything looks as perfect as it does in textbooks. In real life, samples can vary a lot, and you have to be prepared for that. This made the experience feel more realistic and practical compared to just studying from slides.

## Skills and Understanding

I understood all parts of the urinalysis process clearly, including physical, chemical, and microscopic analysis. I didn't struggle with anything in particular, mainly because the trainer was very helpful and always answered my questions. That made a big difference in building my confidence while working.

## Cases Observed

During my time there, I saw a variety of cases, including many yeast infection samples. I also frequently observed calcium oxalate crystals and amorphous phosphate crystals, which helped reinforce what I had learned in theory. One case that stood out to me was seeing a renal tubular epithelial cell, which was interesting because it's not something we always see in practice. Experiences like this helped me connect what I learned in class to real patient samples.

Correlating results with conditions was actually pretty easy for me, especially because the PowerPoint materials provided were very helpful. They made it easier to remember patterns and apply them during interpretation. I didn't feel like I made any major mistakes, which also helped build my confidence.

## Key Lesson

After this experience, I definitely feel more confident in interpreting urinalysis results and not missing important details. One important thing I learned is that some urine samples may look clear and normal, but can still contain significant findings. Because of this, it's very important to carefully review the results from the Sysmex UD instrument and then confirm by spinning the sample and examining it microscopically. This attention to detail is really important to avoid missing anything.

## Reflection

Overall, this experience will be very helpful for my future as a medical laboratory professional. It gave me exposure to real hospital settings and the types of patients seen there, especially in areas like labor and delivery. It also showed me how important it is to interpret results carefully and decide whether they are valid or need to be repeated. I think this hands on experience will definitely help me in the future when working independently in a lab.
